Our Projects

A showcase of the creativity and hard work from our members.

Autonomous Delivery Drone

This project, developed by the Aerodrone Club, is an autonomous quadcopter capable of navigating a predefined GPS path to deliver a small payload. It features custom-built hardware and a flight controller running open-source software.

Team: Aarav Patel, Sneha Reddy, Jaspreet Singh

AI-Powered Robotic Arm

A project from the Build Club that combines robotics and machine learning. This 5-axis robotic arm uses a camera and a neural network to identify and sort objects by color and shape, showcasing the practical application of AI.

Team: Priya Sharma, Rohan Gupta, Fatima Khan
